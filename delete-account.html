Delete Your Lzaz Account

You can permanently delete your Lzaz Sports account and all associated data directly from the app.

Step 1 — Open the Lzaz Sports app
Step 2 — Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner
Step 3 — Go to Settings
Step 4 — Tap Delete Account
Step 5 — Confirm deletion

Your account and all personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

To request deletion by email: support@lzazsports.com
