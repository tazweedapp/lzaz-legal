Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Lzaz Technology Company ("Lzaz", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Lzaz Sports mobile application. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and username when you register.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location when you create or search for matches nearby. Location is only accessed with your permission.
  • Usage data: match history, team activity, connections, and in-app interactions.
  • Device data: device type, OS version, and push notification tokens for delivering alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the app and provide core features (match creation, player connections, team management).
  • To send push notifications about matches, invitations, and updates you have opted into.
  • To improve the app through aggregated, anonymised analytics.
  • To enforce our community guidelines and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Other users as part of normal app functionality (e.g. your profile is visible to players you connect with).
  • Cloud service providers (AWS, MongoDB Atlas) that host our infrastructure under strict data-processing agreements.
  • Firebase (Google) for push notification delivery.

4. Location Permissions

Location access is used solely to show nearby matches and venues. We do not share your precise location with other users. You may revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the app, which permanently removes your personal data within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Lzaz Sports is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (TLS), hashed passwords, and access controls to protect your data.

8. Your Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
